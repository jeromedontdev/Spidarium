Fix the stuck duplicate gift, give gifts layer control, unify pixel density

Three reports: changing the gift left two on screen with one pinned to the
middle, gifts still had no layer settings, and the large decor had visibly
smaller pixels than the props and gifts.

The duplicate gift was a preview image left visible while the gift layer stayed
hidden. terrarium_preview_hide() only unhid the layer when s_preview_gift was
set, so once that flag had been cleared the layer stayed hidden while the
preview image -- a SIBLING of the layer, not a child -- remained on screen. The
unhide is now unconditional and the commit path hides the preview before
refreshing the gift.

The gift's layer is its position among the decor in its band. The gift is a
single item, so ordering it against other gifts would be meaningless; what the
player arranges is the gift against the decor. decor_gift_layer_count/index/
set_layer treat it as one more member of the band sequence and the renderer
inserts it at that index. This is a new save field, v22: one byte, and the
struct is 4-byte aligned, so the payload grew 3100 -> 3104. payload_prefix()
had to stop returning sizeof(game_state_t) for v18..v21 or every older save
would have been rejected as short.

All art now shares one pixel density. A gift is a 64x64 source drawn at scale
512, so one source pixel is a 2x2 block on screen. The climb structures were
192x192 drawn 1:1, half that density, so their pixels looked smaller. They are
now 96x96 drawn at scale 512, and import_climb.py snaps the result to the 2x2
grid so asset_build.py can enforce the same uniformity rule it applies to every
other sprite. The climb exemption from that rule is gone. All six were
regenerated through nano banana rather than downscaled, so the detail is
authored at the right scale.
