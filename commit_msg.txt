Stylise the climb art, tilt the body with the ramp, stop shadowing the structure

Three faults reported on the large climb decor: some pieces were almost too
realistic, the spider cast a shadow on the item it was climbing, and there was
no perspective shift.

The shadow was drawn only while climbing, which was backwards. The surface
under a climbing spider is the structure itself, so a shadow at the floor point
landed on the very thing being climbed. It is now drawn only on the floor.

The body now tilts with the surface. decor_climb_slope() reports the ramp
gradient as a signed -1..1 value and the renderer rotates the whole body by
atan(slope) * CLIMB_TILT_FOLLOW, clamped to CLIMB_TILT_MAX. The forward axis
also compresses toward CLIMB_FORESHORTEN as the lift approaches
CLIMB_FULL_LIFT, so the viewing angle changes with height. A level spider on a
ramp reads as a sprite sliding up the screen, which was the report.

The art is regenerated with simplification rules rather than a style reminder.
The first set failed because the prompts described materials photographically
("deep parallel grain lines", "sun-bleached silvery patches"). SIMPLIFY_RULE
and SURFACE_RULE now cap each material at three or four flat tones, forbid
texture noise and fine hatching, and require the walkable top to be a calm band
of flat colour.
